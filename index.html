About

I am a Lecturer at Capital University of Economics and Business, where I have worked since July 2020. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Capital Normal University, advised by Professor Guohua Zou.

My research centers on model averaging and statistical methods for complex data, with work on treatment effect estimation, heterogeneous treatment effects, and high-dimensional longitudinal data.

Research

I study methods that combine information across models to improve estimation in complex statistical settings.
